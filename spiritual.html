Spiritual Space

Interactive stories rooted in faith - told with love and respect.

Some stories are meant to be felt, not just read. This is a quiet corner for interactive experiences built around faith, values, and the moments that shape a good heart - made especially for young ones to learn, choose, and grow.

The Lantern of Sabr

On the night after Ashura, Lady Zainab (peace be upon her) gathers the frightened children of the camp to the light. A reverent, non-violent scene about patience through pain - move slowly, calm each fearful heart, and leave no one in the dark.

Atmospheric · One-minute scene · A game of sabr (patience) · Told with reverence

The Night of the Cloak

An interactive story for children about the event of Hadith al-Kisa. Choose kindly, watch the light grow, and earn all five badges. Told with love and respect.

For kids · Story with choices · 5 chapters · Badge collection
