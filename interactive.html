Interactive Space

Playable experiments in game design - built for the browser.

Writing about game design is one thing. Shipping playable things is another. This is where I put small games and interactive experiments - each one built around a single mechanic, polished until it feels right. No downloads, no installs. Click and play.

Lightkeeper

A girl, a lantern, and a world of living shadows. Running feeds the dark - standing firm burns it away. A survival game about steadfastness.

Keyboard + touch · Works on mobile · Courage costs light

Orbit

A one-button game of gravity and timing. Release, fly, get captured by the next planet. Tight orbits score double. How far up can you climb?

One button · Works on mobile · Best played with sound off and focus on
