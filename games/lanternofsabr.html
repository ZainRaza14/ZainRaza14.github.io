The Lantern of Sabr
The Long Night · A Tale of Patience

A storm of fire swept through the village in the night, and the people fled. Now the dark lies heavy over the broken homes, and the children are scattered and afraid. Layla takes up the last lantern and walks into the night to gather them, one by one, to the light.

Move with arrows / WASD, or hold and drag on a touch screen.
Bring your lantern close to a frightened child and hold it there until their fear softens. They will follow you.
Do not rush. Fear cannot be outrun — only calmed. Move too fast and those behind you are lost to the dark again. Walk with patience, and lead them all to the safe tent's glow.

This is a fictional story. Its people are shown only as figures of light and shadow - a small tale to remember that patience is a light we carry for others.
