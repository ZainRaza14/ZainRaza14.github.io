Sabr — Patience
The Lantern of Sabr
شَامِ الغَريبَان · The Night of the Strangers

The fires have gone cold. On the night after Ashura, the camp lies in darkness, and the children are scattered and afraid. Lady Zainab, peace be upon her, takes up a lantern and goes into the dark to gather them, one by one, to the light.

Move with arrows / WASD, or hold and drag on a touch screen.
Bring your lantern close to a frightened child and hold it there until their fear softens. They will follow you.
Do not rush. Fear cannot be outrun — only calmed. Move too fast and those behind you are lost to the dark again. Walk with patience, and lead them all to the safe tent's glow.

Out of love and reverence, Lady Zainab and the children are shown only as veiled figures of light and shadow. May peace be upon her and the family of the Prophet.
