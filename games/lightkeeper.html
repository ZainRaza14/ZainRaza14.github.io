LIGHTKEEPER

The dark came for her village, and everyone ran.
She picked up the last lantern instead.

Move with WASD / arrows or touch-drag. Gather the falling embers to keep her lantern burning.
When the shadows lunge, running feeds them - they grow faster in pursuit.
Hold SPACE (or press with a second finger) to stand firm: she plants her feet
and her light flares, burning every shadow that dares come close.

Courage costs light. Light is life. How long can she hold the dark back?

Tap or press space to light the lantern
